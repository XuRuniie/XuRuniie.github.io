Runjie Xu

I’m an undergraduate student at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Nanjing University.

My research interests lie in reinforcement learning, world models, and embodied intelligence. I’m particularly interested in using learned world models to make policy learning and decision-making more efficient for embodied agents.
